01 / Why it matters

Your voice stays yours.

With the complete edition and its locally installed models, your recordings and text are processed on your device. They are not sent to a remote service for transcription or writing.

You need Internet once to download the models. After that, local processing works offline. If you intentionally configure an external provider in advanced settings, that provider’s privacy terms apply.

05 / What does your computer need?

What does your computer need?

No subscription or separate server for the complete edition. These are practical guidelines, not guaranteed minimums for every system.

To transcribe

A recent 64-bit computer, microphone for live recording and available disk space for the speech model.

To write structured reports

16 GB RAM is recommended for comfortable use of the local writing model. Lower-spec computers may be slower.

To make it faster

A compatible graphics card can speed things up. Without a dedicated GPU, the app can fall back to the processor, which takes longer.

Keep several gigabytes of storage free for the models and their files. Exact requirements depend on your system and selected models. New complete editions include document exporters without installing Pandoc or LibreOffice separately.

06 / FAQ

A few simple answers.

Does my audio go to the Internet?+

Not when you use the local models in the complete edition. Audio and text are processed on your device. The first model download needs Internet; external providers remain an optional advanced choice.

Do I need to understand AI?+

No. Install the complete edition, let it set up the local models on first launch, then record or import audio.

What if I do not have a powerful graphics card?+

The processor can do the work, only more slowly. For structured reports, 16 GB RAM is recommended.

Can I use it for medical consultations?+

Yes. Dental and medical profiles help structure your notes. You can review and edit the report inside ScribeWatch before exporting and adding it to your clinical software. The clinician retains final approval.

Does it work without Wi-Fi?+

The first run needs Internet to download the models. Once those are installed, local recordings and reports can be processed offline.

For clinical documents, you stay in control: review, edit and approve the report before adding it to a patient record.
